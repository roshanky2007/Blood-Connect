Important Donor Eligibility & Compatibility Notice

BloodConnect connects requesters with voluntary potential donors based on self-reported blood group information. This application does not verify or certify medical compatibility or donor fitness. Official compatibility testing, infectious disease screening, and donor eligibility checks must be conducted by qualified medical staff at certified blood banks.
