Important Medical & Compatibility Notice

BloodConnect is a donor discovery and communication platform, not a medical compatibility or blood bank management system. Search results do not confirm donor eligibility or compatibility. Final donor eligibility and cross-matching must always be conducted and verified by licensed healthcare professionals and authorized blood banks.

How BloodConnect Works

A transparent 5-step workflow connecting patients, voluntary donors, and healthcare centers safely.

01

Register as Donor or Requester

Create your secure profile in seconds. Specify your blood group and location as a voluntary donor, or sign up as a requester to post urgent hospital requirements.

02

Search Compatible Donors

Filter available donors across all blood groups (A+, B+, O+, AB+, etc.) by city, state, or PIN code with complete privacy protections for personal contact info.

03

Submit or Respond to Requests

Requesters broadcast emergency needs specifying hospital details and urgency levels. Registered donors matching the blood group receive immediate notification.

04

Connect with Potential Donors

Matching voluntary donors accept requests with an estimated arrival time. Direct verified contact coordinates unlock safely for rapid coordination.

05

Track Request Status

Monitor real-time progress from Pending to Accepted and Fulfilled. Coordinate hospital arrival safely and mark requests complete once donation succeeds.
